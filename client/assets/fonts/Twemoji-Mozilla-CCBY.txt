Twemoji-Mozilla — attribution and licence
==========================================

The HayChat client embeds `Twemoji-Mozilla.ttf` (with Odin's `#load`) as
the colour-emoji fallback for the Inter UI font.

Artwork
-------
The emoji glyphs are derived from **Twemoji** by Twitter, Inc. and
contributors. Twemoji artwork is licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC-BY-4.0).

  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Required attribution: "Twemoji by Twitter, Inc. and contributors —
CC-BY 4.0 — https://twemoji.twitter.com".

Every HayChat client binary redistributes this artwork, so this
attribution must ship with the client (for example in its credits or
documentation).

Build
-----
This is the **Twemoji-Mozilla** build: a TTF assembling Twemoji's SVG
sources into COLRv0 layered glyphs. The build tooling is maintained by
Mozilla under the Apache 2.0 licence at:

  https://github.com/mozilla/twemoji-colr

The artwork licence (CC-BY-4.0) governs redistribution; the tool
licence (Apache 2.0) only matters if the font is regenerated.
